Privacy Policy for Cyrex

Effective Date: January 15, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Cyrex ("we," "our," or "us," operated by developer PAYNUL ALI) provides a risk-free cryptocurrency paper trading and learning simulator. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data practices. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when using the mobile application Cyrex - Crypto Paper Trading (package com.cyrex.cyrex, the "App") and our companion websites.

Key Summary: Cyrex is strictly a virtual paper trading simulator intended for adults (18+). You can use the app in Guest Mode without entering an email address, using a pseudonymous Firebase Anonymous identifier for server-side simulation state. If you optionally link Google Sign-In or participate in public leaderboards and division leagues, your public trader handle and simulated performance stats are visible to other players and synced securely via Google Firebase and our Oracle Cloud settlement server.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

1.1 Guest Mode (Pseudonymous Sessions)

You can use Cyrex in Guest Mode without providing your name, email address, or phone number. Guest Mode assigns a pseudonymous Firebase Anonymous User ID (UID) so our backend settlement server and Cloud Firestore can preserve your virtual balances, positions, limit orders, and league pod standing across sessions, while UI preferences and local caches are stored on your device in Android SharedPreferences.

1.2 Optional Google Sign-In, Public Leaderboards & Division Leagues

To prevent losing progress across device changes or reinstalls, Cyrex offers optional Google Sign-In via Google OAuth 2.0 and Firebase Authentication:

  • Authentication Identifiers: When linking Google, we receive standard account identifiers provided by Google (email address, display name, and Firebase UID).
  • Public Leaderboards, Division Leagues & Whale Intel: When you set a trader nickname (with at least one trade) or join a seasonal/weekly competition, your public handle, selected avatar index, optional country flag code (derived solely from the device's selected system locale to render a national flag, never from precise GPS/location tracking), league/prestige tier, win rate, trade count, and simulated equity/profit/ROI metrics are stored in Cloud Firestore and displayed publicly to other App users in Global Leaderboards, Division League pods, and Top Trader / Whale Intel views.

1.3 Push Notifications, Server Settlement & Device Alerts

To execute 24/7 simulated stop-loss, take-profit, limit-order fills, and custom price alerts while the App is closed, Cyrex processes your active simulated positions, pending limit orders, custom price alerts, login streak counter, device timezone identifier (e.g., Asia/Jakarta or UTC), and optional leaderboard country flag code on the Cyrex backend settlement server. If you grant notification permissions, a randomized Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token is also sent to our backend server solely to deliver real-time trade settlement notifications, price alerts, daily login streak reminders, and division league results during appropriate local daytime hours. FCM tokens and timezone identifiers do not reveal your personal identity.

1.4 In-App Feedback & Diagnostics

If you voluntarily submit feedback or a bug report inside the App, we collect the message text, chosen feedback category, optional star rating, app version, device OS platform, nickname, and your Firebase UID inside the Cloud Firestore app_feedback collection solely to troubleshoot issues and improve the App.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

To provide real-time market data, 24/7 simulated trade settlement, cloud synchronization, diagnostics, and monetization, Cyrex uses the following infrastructure and trusted third-party SDKs:

  • Cyrex Settlement & Market Feed Server (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure): Hosts our dedicated backend daemon and market data proxy (cyrex-feed.duckdns.org). It processes simulated trading orders, evaluates 24/7 stop-loss/take-profit/liquidation triggers against live market feeds, maintains division league pods, and dispatches FCM push alerts. All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure user authentication (Anonymous & Google Sign-In), Cloud Firestore database storage, Firebase Crashlytics crash diagnostics, FCM push notifications, and Google Analytics for Firebase. Learn more: Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (Google LLC): Serves interstitial and rewarded advertisements (Cyrex does not serve sticky or banner advertisements). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic telemetry to prevent fraud and measure ads. Where required (such as EEA/UK/Switzerland), Cyrex presents Google's UMP consent prompt, and you can review or update your consent choices anytime via Profile > Ad & Data Privacy or Android Settings > Google > Ads. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.
  • Public Market Data & Asset CDNs: Real-time cryptocurrency prices and candles come from Binance public market endpoints (proxied cleanly with no user credentials sent). Token logos are fetched over HTTPS from public asset CDNs (cryptologos.cc and assets.coincap.io), which receive standard HTTP network requests without user account details.

3. Paper Trading & Financial Simulation Disclaimer

Cyrex is an educational cryptocurrency trading simulator. All funds, cash balances, margin positions, and profits displayed within the App are 100% virtual and have no real-world monetary value. Cyrex does not accept deposits of real fiat currency or cryptocurrency, does not execute real financial transactions on any blockchain or exchange, and does not link to bank accounts or real cryptocurrency wallets.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

  • Retention Period: We retain your simulated portfolio, server ledger, and profile data only as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion. De-identified telemetry handled by Google Analytics, Crashlytics, and AdMob is aged out per Google's retention schedules.
  • Local Device Data: You can wipe all local application caches and preferences at any time by clearing storage in your Android Settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Account & Server Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance): Both Guest (Anonymous UID) and Google-linked users can permanently delete their account and associated server ledger, positions, orders, price alerts, notification watch records, nickname reservation, active division pod membership, leaderboard entries, and Firebase Authentication identity at any time directly inside the App via Profile > Account Controls > Delete Account & Data, by following our web deletion portal at https://cyrex-29575.firebaseapp.com/delete-account, or by emailing gamearcade199@gmail.com. Verified remote email requests (including optional erasure of previously submitted feedback) are permanently purged within 30 days.

5. Adult Audience & Children's Privacy

Cyrex is intended strictly for users aged 18 and older due to simulated leveraged trading and game-style reward mechanics, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from minors or children under the age of 13. If you believe a child or minor has submitted personal information via Google Sign-In or feedback, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

6. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. All communication between the App, Google Firebase services, and our Oracle Cloud settlement server (cyrex-feed.duckdns.org) is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Backend endpoints enforce Firebase Authentication token verification, and Cloud Firestore access is restricted by Firestore Security Rules.
