1. How to Delete Your Account Inside the App (Instant Deletion)

If you have the Cyrex mobile app installed on your Android device, you can trigger an instant, automated purge of your account (Google-linked or Guest UID) directly within the app without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open the Cyrex app.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Controls section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. In the confirmation dialog ("Delete Account & Data?"), tap Delete Permanently.

Outcome: Your account is signed out immediately. Our backend settlement server (cyrex-feed.duckdns.org on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure) and Firebase client purge your server account ledger, active positions, limit orders, price alerts, notification watch records, nickname reservation, active Division League pod membership, seasonal and all-time leaderboard entries, and Firebase Authentication identity, while clearing local device storage.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

4.1 Can I recover my trading history after deleting my account?

No. Deletion is instantaneous and permanent. Cloud backups, leaderboard rankings, and virtual portfolios cannot be recovered under any circumstance.

4.2 What if I used Guest Mode without signing in with Google?

Guest Mode still assigns an anonymous Firebase User ID (UID) so 24/7 stop-loss/take-profit execution, price alerts, and Division Leagues work on our settlement server. You can permanently delete that guest server account and local storage at any time inside the app via Profile > Account Controls > Delete Account & Data, or clear app storage / uninstall to erase local device state.

4.3 Does account deletion cost anything?

No. Account and data deletion is completely free of charge both inside the app and via email requests.
